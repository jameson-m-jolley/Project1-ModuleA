this means that when you pass 128 as an argument, 
it is being interpreted as a negative number (-128) 
instead of a positive number (128). 
this can cause unexpected behavior in your program. to fix this, 
you can either change the type of the variable that stores the argument to unsigned char,
or you can cast the argument to unsigned char when you pass it to the function.
